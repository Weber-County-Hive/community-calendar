🔥 Transparency: Weber Fire District and your property tax

  • A New Line on the Bill: Roy and Riverdale's proposed move into Weber Fire District. It covers what the district says the change would cost an average home, and the one question the records don't answer yet: how each city's required rate cut will be calculated.
  • Paying for the Next Station: how the district is paying for its growth. That includes the 23.58% levy increase approved in December 2025 (an extra $2,956,000 a year, passed 9–1), the lease revenue bond for new stations in West Haven and Hooper and a training center, and the Nordic Village and Powder Mountain agreements.
  • Every Weber County Property Tax Increase Since 2019: Weber County taxing entities proposed 59 increases for tax years 2020 through 2026, and at least 46 were adopted. The list is checked against the Utah State Tax Commission's approved-rate reports.

📜 Bill-Tracker

  • S.B. 206 (2026), grade C: it started as an appraisal bill and ended as Utah's first statewide ledger for tax increment. Every existing project area has to be filed in one database by Jan. 1, 2027. A House committee amendment changed when the new rules apply: at the trigger itself, instead of the process leading up to it.
  • H.B. 276 (2026), grade C: a new right to sue over AI-made intimate images, and content-origin labels. The final version has no minimum damages for the people depicted and adds protections for platforms.
