⚖️ Same rules for everyone: two grading changes

Money. Every candidate's money is now graded on one fixed scale, whatever the party. The grade is based on the share of a campaign's money that came from individual people.

  • A: 90% or more
  • B: 67–89%
  • C: 34–66%
  • D: 10–33%
  • F: under 10%

Campaigns under $1,000 get N/A. A candidate's own money is stated on the page but not graded. 22 race pages now have a Can they compete? panel that shows every candidate's money side by side.

Disclosure forms. Missing items are now graded with a ladder, so the same gap gets the same grade on every page:

  • One item voters can't find anywhere else left off the form (a company owned, a board seat, a job): D.
  • Two of those items left off: D−.
  • Three or more left off: F.
  • Only legislative pay left off: D+.
  • No form on file: F.
  • Optional items listed, such as a spouse's employer: B.

Anything named anywhere on the form counts as listed.
