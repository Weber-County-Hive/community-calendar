Send us a date

Know of an election date, public hearing or deadline in Morgan County? Send it in.

webercountyhive@gmail.com

Include the date, time, place and a link if there is one (a Facebook event, a flyer, a city or county notice). We check each date before it goes up, and we may write back to ask if you’d like to tell us more.

How this calendar works

  • Every date links to where it came from.
  • Dates from Hive case files link back to the case file.
  • Tap Add to my calendar to save a date to your phone, or Share to send it to a friend or post it on Facebook.
  • Dates marked Date not final may change. Check the source before you go.
